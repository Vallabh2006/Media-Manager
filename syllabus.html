Course Curriculum

Syllabus

Explore the key educational units covered under the AI for Everyone curriculum.

Unit 1: Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals

Understand basic AI terminology, history, limitations, and potential applications shaping modern industry and society.

  • AI definition & timelines
  • Weak vs Strong AI
  • Turing Test & Algorithms

Unit 2: Machine Learning Basics

Introduction to data training, supervised and unsupervised learning, simple model training, and basic neural networks.

  • Supervised learning models
  • Unsupervised clustering
  • Neural Network structure

Unit 3: Generative AI & LLMs

Deep-dive into modern Large Language Models, Generative Adversarial Networks, and text-to-image foundation models.

  • Transformer Architecture
  • Text & image generation
  • Creative AI applications

Unit 4: Prompt Engineering

Mastering interactive strategies for Large Language Models. Zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought prompting.

  • Few-shot examples
  • Role prompting styles
  • Chain-of-Thought flows

Unit 5: AI Productivity Tools

Practical usage of AI companions, search systems, summarization, and automation utilities for daily workflow.

  • Document summaries
  • Code assistants (Copilot)
  • Information synthesis

Unit 6: Ethics & Responsible AI

Addressing bias in models, data privacy constraints, copyright conflicts, and building ethical guidelines for AI creation.

  • Bias mitigation & fairness
  • Data sovereignty laws
  • Academic integrity guidelines
